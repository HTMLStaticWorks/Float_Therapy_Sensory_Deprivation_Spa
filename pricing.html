Got Questions?

Pricing & Membership FAQ

Click any question below to reveal detailed answers regarding our memberships, rollover credits, and session inclusions.

Can I pause or cancel my membership anytime?

Yes! Zenith Monthly Memberships can be paused or canceled directly from your guest dashboard with zero penalty fees before your next scheduled billing date.

What happens to my unused float credits?

Unused float credits from 3-packs and monthly memberships remain active and automatically roll over for 90 days. You can also share credits with friends and family.

Are gift float vouchers available?

Absolutely. You can purchase single float passes, 3-pack bundle gift vouchers, or customized sanctuary certificates directly online or through our front-desk concierge team.

What is included with my float session?

Every float session includes a private luxury suite, rain shower, organic sulfate-free shampoo & body wash, silicone earplugs, plush towels, hair dryers, and post-float herbal tea lounge access.

Do I need to bring anything with me?

Nothing at all! We provide all amenities, towels, and body care products. Floating is experienced without swimwear in your private locked suite, but you are welcome to wear a swimsuit if you prefer.
