Step 01

Arrival & Personal Prep

Welcome to our silent sanctuary. Your private guide walks you through your custom float suite, demonstrates light and music controls, and provides soft silicone earplugs and natural organic toiletries.

Effortless Zero-Gravity Floating

Step into warm, buoyant water saturated with 1,000 lbs of pure Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate). The water supports your body effortlessly — removing pressure from spinal discs, joints, and tense muscles.
