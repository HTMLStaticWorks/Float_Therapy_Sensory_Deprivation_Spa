First-Time Floater Guide

Your Complete Guide to Zero Gravity

Step-by-step preparation, pod customization, and sensory deprivation science for a serene first float.

Step-By-Step Preparation

What to Expect During Your First Session

1

Pre-Float Shower & Earplugs

Inside your private luxury suite, insert the provided silicone earplugs and take a warm shower to rinse away perfumes, lotions, and hair product.

2

Step Into Weightless Water

Step into the float pod or open suite. The 10-inch water depth is saturated with 1,000 lbs of Epsom salt, allowing you to float effortlessly on your back.

3

Set Your Ambient Lighting & Sound

You hold full control. Turn off the pod light for pure darkness or leave the soft starry sky ceiling on with gentle ambient music.

4

Post-Float Refreshment Lounge

Soft music signals session completion. Take a refreshing shower to rinse away mineral salts, then relax in our lounge with herbal tea.

Sanctuary Standards

Our Purity & Hygiene Promise

Four uncompromising pillars ensuring pristine water quality and absolute floater comfort

1,000 Lbs Medical Epsom Salt

USP-grade magnesium sulfate provides maximum buoyancy, relaxing tight muscles and reducing systemic inflammation.

100% Water Filtration

Between every single guest float, 100% of the water passes through 1-micron filters, high-output UV light, and ozone purification.

Skin-Temperature Match

Water is maintained at an exact skin temperature of 93.5°F (34.2°C) so your body forgets where skin ends and water begins.

Private Luxury Suites

Every float room is 100% private and equipped with its own rain shower, plush towels, earplugs, and natural organic toiletries.
