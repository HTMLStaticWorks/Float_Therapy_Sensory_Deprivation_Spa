THE SOLACE PHILOSOPHY

Reclaiming Stillness in an Overstimulated Society

In our modern everyday lives, the human nervous system is under unceasing demand. Artificial blue light, digital notifications, urban acoustic clutter, and the constant downward drag of gravity create persistent low-grade stress and chronic musculoskeletal tension.

Solace was founded on a simple yet profound realization: when you subtract the external sensory barrage and remove gravitational pressure from the spine, the human body and consciousness possess an innate, extraordinary capacity to repair and rebalance.

SANCTUARY STANDARDS

Our Uncompromising Hygiene Promise

Our water purification and sanitation protocols exceed municipal swimming pools and medical recovery centers.

1,000 Lbs Pure USP Salt

High salinity creates a natural saline environment where pathogenic bacteria and microbes simply cannot survive.

100% Water Turnover

High-intensity germicidal UV-C light combined with dissolved active ozone neutralizes 99.9% of organics and micro-particles.

HEPA Fresh Air Inflow

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About Floating

Common questions about safety, comfort, and the float experience at Solace

What if I am prone to claustrophobia or anxiety?
You are always in complete control. Our pods feature interior light switches, audio controls, and an easy-lift door that remains open as wide as you like. Additionally, we offer Horizon Open Basin Suites with high ceilings and spacious surroundings for first-timers who prefer an open environment.
How is the float water cleaned between sessions?
Between every single guest, 100% of the water passes 3 to 5 times through a 1-micron sediment core filter, high-output germicidal UV-C light, and advanced dissolved ozone purification. The 1,000 lbs salt saturation also creates a natural saline environment where bacteria cannot survive.
Can I float if I don't know how to swim?
Yes, absolutely! The 1,000 lbs of Epsom salt makes the water far denser than the Dead Sea (specific gravity 1.28). It is physically impossible to sink — you will effortlessly float on the surface like a cork without any effort or swimming ability required.
What should I bring for my session?
Nothing at all! We provide everything inside your private luxury suite: warm rain showers, organic sulfate-free shampoo, conditioner, body wash, makeup wipes, plush towels, earplugs, and blow dryers. You can float in swimwear or completely nude for true sensory freedom.
What happens if I fall asleep while floating?
Many floaters drift into gentle, deeply restorative Theta sleep. Because of the extreme buoyancy, your face remains comfortably above the water line at all times. Gentle chime music will softly wake you at the conclusion of your session.
